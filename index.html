About Sama Link

Built for Saudi organisations

Sama Link is a Riyadh-based technology solutions company and a member of Sama Group of Companies. We design, build and support the infrastructure organisations run on — networks, data centres, security systems and the Microsoft cloud — and we use AI automation to make that technology work harder for the business.

Backed by a group with roots in the Saudi market since 1980 and companies across travel, healthcare, defence, maintenance and more, we bring enterprise-grade engineering with the accountability of one local partner.

Network & Data Centre

Networks, Wi-Fi, firewalls and data centres, built from the ground up.

  • Campus switching, routing and enterprise Wi-Fi
  • Firewalls, SD-WAN and secure remote access
  • IP telephony and unified communications
  • Structured cabling — copper and fibre
  • Turnkey data centres and server rooms: raised floors, cooling, power, racks and containment

Security & Surveillance

CCTV and access control that protect people, premises and assets.

  • IP CCTV, recorders and video management
  • Access control — card, PIN and biometric
  • Site survey and coverage design before installation
  • PoE, segmented networks and secure remote viewing
  • Clean installation, maintenance and expansion

Microsoft Cloud & Modern Workplace

Microsoft 365, Azure and licensing — deployed, migrated and secured.

  • Microsoft 365 and Azure licensing, renewals handled
  • Migrations from Google Workspace, Exchange and other platforms
  • Entra ID, multi-factor authentication and conditional access
  • Teams and SharePoint structured around how your people work
  • Azure servers and hybrid connectivity, cost-optimised

Managed IT Services

Users, devices and servers administered day to day by our team.

  • Microsoft 365 and user administration: onboarding, licences, mailboxes and permissions
  • Devices enrolled in Intune, policies enforced and patches applied
  • Windows Server and Active Directory: accounts, group policy, file and print
  • Requests prioritised by business impact, with response times agreed in every contract
