Network & Data Centre

Networks, Wi-Fi, firewalls and data centres, built from the ground up.

  • Campus switching, routing and enterprise Wi-Fi
  • Firewalls, perimeter security, SD-WAN and secure remote access
  • IP telephony and unified communications
  • Structured cabling — copper and fibre
  • Turnkey data centres and server rooms: raised floors, cooling, power, racks and aisle containment

Security & Surveillance

CCTV and access control that protect people, premises and assets.

  • IP CCTV: HD cameras, recorders and video management
  • Access control — card, PIN and biometric entry for doors, gates and restricted areas
  • Site survey and coverage design before anything is installed
  • PoE, segmented networks and secure remote viewing on authorised devices
  • Clean installation, maintenance and expansion as your site grows

Microsoft Cloud & Modern Workplace

Microsoft 365, Azure and licensing — deployed, migrated and secured.

  • Microsoft 365 and Azure licensing, renewals handled
  • Migrations from Google Workspace, Exchange and other platforms with minimal disruption
  • Entra ID, multi-factor authentication and conditional access
  • Laptops and phones enrolled, secured and updated through Intune
  • Teams, SharePoint, Azure servers and hybrid connectivity

Managed IT Services

Your IT, administered by experts — so your team stays focused on the business.

  • Microsoft 365 and users: onboarding, offboarding, licences, mailboxes and permissions
  • Devices and patching: Intune enrolment, policies enforced, updates applied
  • Servers and directory: Windows Server and Active Directory, group policy, file and print
  • Proactive support: requests prioritised by business impact, with response times agreed in every contract
